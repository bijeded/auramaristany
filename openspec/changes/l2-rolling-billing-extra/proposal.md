## Why

CuarentaMás Extra is billed as a fixed six-month term ([002_seed_programs_variants.sql:8](../../../supabase/migrations/002_seed_programs_variants.sql)) but is sold as an open-ended monthly subscription: a client finishes the six-month CuarentaMás, subscribes to Extra at the level she qualifies for, and stays as long as she wants. Only the admin label was corrected when this was noticed ([admin/content/page.tsx:52](../../../app/admin/content/page.tsx) says so out loud); the underlying model never changed.

Closing that gap surfaced two live defects in the fixed-term path itself, both of which have to be resolved here because this change is what makes them reachable.

**Fixed-term subscriptions are never actually ended.** `shouldComplete` writes a `completed_at` timestamp and nothing else ([stripe-handlers.ts:203-205](../../../lib/webhooks/stripe-handlers.ts)) — it does not set `status`, and it does not cancel the Stripe subscription. All ten prices are monthly `recurring`, so **a CuarentaMás client is charged in month 7, month 8, and onward, indefinitely, against content that ended at month 6.** Charging for undelivered service is the most serious thing found in this exploration.

**`status = 'completed'` is unwritable and unreachable.** `SubscriptionStatus` includes it ([types.ts:2](../../../lib/supabase/types.ts)) and the checkout prerequisite gate requires it ([prerequisites.ts:32](../../../lib/subscriptions/prerequisites.ts)), but the CHECK constraint does not permit it ([001_initial_schema.sql:146](../../../supabase/migrations/001_initial_schema.sql)) and no code path writes it. A client who finishes CuarentaMás and tries to buy Extra is told *"No cumples los prerequisitos para este programa"* — permanently. The exact transition this change exists to enable is blocked today. Same enum-vs-CHECK mismatch also applies to `trialing`.

## What Changes

- **The billing rule, stated plainly.** A **CuarentaMás** client stops being charged when she finishes month 6 — the program has a defined end and the platform must enforce it. A **CuarentaMás Extra** or **Strong & Fit** client keeps being charged every month until *she* cancels; there is no end to enforce. `billing_model` is the discriminator and it must be correct for all three programs, which today it is not.
- **`cuarenta-mas-extra` becomes `rolling_monthly`** with `duration_months = null`, matching Strong & Fit. Content past month 6 is handled by the ladder from `l2-level-ladder-progression`; this change only moves the billing model.
- **Fixed-term completion actually completes.** On the final paid month of a `fixed_term_monthly` program: set `status = 'completed'`, keep `completed_at`, and cancel the Stripe subscription **at period end** so no further invoice is raised. The client keeps portal access through the period she paid for and then falls out of `ACCESS_STATES` — which is correct, she has finished and her next step is Extra.

  **Timing (verified against the current increment path).** Stripe bills upfront and `checkout.session.completed` seeds `months_elapsed: 1` without the `subscription_create` invoice incrementing it, so `months_elapsed` reaches 6 as period 6 *begins*. Completion therefore fires at the start of the client's final month: `cancel_at_period_end` lets month 6 play out in full and suppresses the month-7 invoice. Cancelling immediately instead would cut her final paid month short; waiting for the next `invoice.paid` would charge her for a seventh. Neither is acceptable — the period-end cancellation is the only correct option, and this off-by-one must be covered by a test.
- **Rolling programs never complete.** `shouldComplete` stays false for `rolling_monthly` regardless of `months_elapsed`, and the ladder's wrap branch must be evaluated only after the fixed-term branch (see `l2-level-ladder-progression` design, Decision 3) so a CuarentaMás client finishes rather than looping.
- **`status` CHECK constraint is extended** to include `completed` and `trialing`, bringing the database in line with `SubscriptionStatus`. Per the project's enum/CHECK rule, the union and the constraint migrate together.
- **A completed client keeps a graduated portal, not a locked door.** `middleware.ts:56` gates the portal on `ACCESS_STATES`, which will not contain `completed` — so making completion real would eject a CuarentaMás client at the precise moment she is meant to subscribe to Extra, and would take her progress history and progress photos with her. Instead, `completed` grants a **reduced tier**: her account, payment history, progress history and progress photos remain reachable, plus a clear CTA to continue with CuarentaMás Extra. **No new training content** — no `/portal/today`, no `/portal/semana`, no pillars.

  The distinction the tier encodes: she keeps what she earned (her own data, permanently) and loses what she was paying for (new content). It also makes the CuarentaMás → Extra handoff a funnel inside the app rather than a round trip through WordPress.

  ⚠ **This widens an access boundary.** `subscriptionGrantsAccess` is documented as the single source of truth for portal access and propagates to middleware, `getTodayContent` and `getPerformanceData`. A second tier must be expressed so that content-serving paths keep using the strict check — adding `completed` to `ACCESS_STATES` wholesale would silently serve training content to clients who are no longer paying for it.
- **The checkout prerequisite gate is removed.** Eligibility for a level is decided by Aura's evaluation on the WordPress site, which then redirects to the appropriate program and level. Enforcing a contradictory rule in SQL blocks exactly the clients she has approved: an evaluated client with no prior CuarentaMás subscription is refused at Extra Avanzado. The gate moves to the funnel; `program_variant_prerequisites` rows are removed for the affected variants.
- **`subscriptionProgressLabel` handles a completed fixed-term subscription** rather than continuing to count months past the program's end.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `subscription-billing-lifecycle`: when a subscription stops billing and when it never does — fixed-term completion and its exact timing, the terminal `completed` status, and the absence of any eligibility gate at checkout.
- `portal-graduated-access`: what a `completed` client can still reach (account, payment history, progress history and photos, the continue-with-Extra CTA) and what she cannot (any training content), plus how that tier stays distinct from the paying-access check.

### Modified Capabilities
- `portal-subscription-management`: the settings page must distinguish a *finished* subscription from a *cancelled* one — completion sets `cancel_at_period_end`, which today would offer a finished client a "Reactivar" button that resumes billing on a programme that has ended.

## Impact

- **Migration 017:** flip `billing_model`/`duration_months` for `cuarenta-mas-extra`; extend the `subscriptions.status` CHECK with `completed` and `trialing`; delete the affected `program_variant_prerequisites` rows.
- **Modified code:** `lib/webhooks/stripe-handlers.ts` (completion writes `status` and cancels at period end) · `app/api/subscriptions/create-checkout/route.ts` (drop the gate) · `lib/subscriptions/prerequisites.ts` (dead once the gate is gone — remove rather than leave unreferenced) · `lib/admin/clients-helpers.ts` · `app/(marketing)/checkout/[variantSlug]/page.tsx` (renders "6 meses" from `duration_months`).
- **Graduated access:** `lib/content/subscription-access.ts` gains a second, explicitly-named tier alongside `subscriptionGrantsAccess` — the strict check keeps its current meaning and content paths keep calling it · `middleware.ts` admits `completed` to the portal but not to content routes · portal shell/nav hides training tabs for the tier · new continue-with-Extra CTA. **Security-sensitive surface** (`RLS`/access boundary + a middleware matcher) — this change should route through `security-review`, not just `code-review`.
- **Depends on `l2-level-ladder-progression`.** Flipping Extra to rolling before the ladder exists moves the content cliff from month 7 of a fixed program to month 7 of a rolling one — the client is still charged with nothing to show. This change ships last.
- **⚠ Existing subscriptions.** Any live Extra subscription is mid-term when the model flips. Decide explicitly whether in-flight subscriptions adopt rolling behavior or are grandfathered; with demo data only, the simple answer is to reset them, but that assumption must be re-verified at implementation time.
- **⚠ Stripe cancellation is an outward-facing side effect.** Cancelling at period end on completion is correct but irreversible per subscription. Verify against a real test-mode subscription before enabling, and confirm the A9 cancellation UI does not present a confusing state for a `completed` subscription.
- **Removing the prerequisite gate widens who can purchase.** Anyone reaching a checkout URL can subscribe at any level. That is the accepted trade-off — the real gate is Aura's evaluation and the funnel that leads to the URL.
